# Moonveil 1.1

- Eight enemy designs now rotate through realm rosters: Moonwisp fox, Sakura slime, Jade kodama, Astral moth, Clockwork lantern, Jade colossus, Ember oni, and Lunar dragon. Every tenth wave uses a dedicated guardian sprite.
- Recruited companions appear beside Yuki in battle. The five human companions have new full-body sprites based on their existing portraits; Koharu joins as a small fox familiar. Each companion has idle motion, an attack animation, and an element-colored projectile effect.
- Windows and fullscreen use one uniform scale for the entire 16:10 canvas. Wider or taller screens receive centered padding, and mouse coordinates use the same scale and offset. Clicking the padding does not activate game controls.
- Existing saved progression and economy formulas remain compatible.
- New art and reference prompts are recorded in `ART_PROMPTS_V1_1.json` and saved under `assets/`.
